Palo Alto HR Analytics Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

The Palo Alto HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive HR analytics project developed to analyze employee engagement, burnout risk, work-life balance, satisfaction, attrition, and career-related trends.

The dashboard provides HR teams and managers with data-driven insights to identify low-engagement employees, understand burnout-prone groups, and support better employee engagement and retention strategies.

🎯 Problem Statement

Organizations often have employee data but lack a unified view of overall employee engagement and workplace health.

This project aims to provide a centralized dashboard to:

Monitor overall employee engagement
Identify employees with high burnout risk
Analyze work-life balance and overtime patterns
Understand satisfaction and engagement trends
Analyze engagement across different roles and career stages
Highlight areas requiring managerial intervention

📂 Dataset

The project uses the IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance dataset.

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:

Job Role
Job Level
Monthly Income
Job Satisfaction
Work-Life Balance
Overtime
Years at Company
Years in Current Role
Attrition
Employee Engagement-related factors
